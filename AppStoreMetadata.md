## Description
ReadForge transforms your PDF documents into private, listenable audiobooks - all on your device. No cloud uploads, no analytics on your content, complete privacy.

### Key Features
- **Offline Processing**: Everything happens on your device - your documents never leave your phone
- **AI-Powered Cleanup**: Smart text cleaning removes headers, footers, and formatting artifacts
- **Background Audio**: Listen while using other apps or when your screen is off
- **Progress Tracking**: Automatically saves your reading progress
- **Multiple Formats**: Support for PDF, EPUB, and plain text files
- **Voice Customization**: Choose from system voices and adjust playback speed
- **Bookmarks**: Mark important sections for easy reference

### Perfect For
- Students reading academic papers
- Professionals reviewing business documents
- Anyone who prefers audio learning
- Commuters who want to read while driving
- People with visual impairments
